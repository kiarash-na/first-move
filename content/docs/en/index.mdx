# Introduction

First Move is a free web app for the first steps of a fifth-generation district heating and cooling (5GDHC) project. It helps municipalities, energy service providers and other project initiators to screen a project area and to develop a business model for a network there.

## Two modules

A project in First Move has two optional modules. You can use one of them or both, and you can turn each one on or off at any time; the data of a module is kept while it is off.

- **Quick Check**: a first assessment of the technical and economic potential of a 5GDHC network for a project area. You enter a few key parameters, and the Quick Check applies a predefined calculation and assessment methodology. The result is one of three levels, Promising, Further analysis required or Not promising, shown together with the main factors behind it.
- **Business Plan**: a workspace to develop a business model for the network in four steps, from the opportunity to the next steps towards implementation. You write in your own words; the Business Plan calculates nothing of its own.

Both modules share the site data, such as the buildings and the energy sources, so you enter them once.

## Your data stays in your browser

There is no account, and your projects are stored only in the browser you use. First Move never receives them. Export a project to keep a copy or to move it to another browser. [Data and privacy](/docs/data-and-privacy) explains the details.

## Where to start

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Start a new project with the Quick Check, the Business Plan or both, or open one of the six examples. Opening an example creates your own copy; the examples use illustrative inputs only.
3. Or import a project file (`.firstmove.json`) that you exported before.

New to 5GDHC? Read [What is 5GDHC?](/docs/what-is-5gdhc) first.

## A screening, not a feasibility study

The Quick Check gives an initial indication, not a definitive feasibility decision. Its parameters and methodology are based on simplified assumptions; actual values may vary with project-specific conditions and should be refined through further analysis. Neither the results nor these pages are engineering advice.
