# AI Plan Score

The AI Plan Score is a separate AI scoring of your Business Plan. It is an opinion on how solid the written plan is, not a calculation and not a feasibility verdict.

## What it reads

It reads the fields of the four Business Plan steps and, when the Quick Check is on and complete, its overall result.

## How it scores

An AI decision model scores each filled field in turns, from the first step to the last.

## Sources

The method follows the challenge material and published research.
