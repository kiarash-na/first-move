# EQ-3.5 · Heat Density

> Heat density relates the total annual heating demand to the estimated network length. This indicator helps assess how much heating demand can be served relative to the length of network required. Higher heat density can improve the economic outlook because more heating demand is served per meter of network.

$$
\rho_{heat} = \frac{Q_{heat,total}}{l_{total}}
$$

| Symbol | Definition (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| $\rho_{heat}$ | heat density of project area in kWh/m |
| $Q_{heat,total}$ | total annual heating demand of all buildings in kWh/a |
| $l_{total}$ | total net length in m |
