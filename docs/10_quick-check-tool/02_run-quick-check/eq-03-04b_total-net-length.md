# EQ-3.4b · Estimated Network Length: total net length

$$
l_{total} = l_{spec} \cdot \frac{A_{proj}}{{10}^{4}}
$$

| Symbol | Definition (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| $l_{total}$ | total net length in m |
| $l_{spec}$ | specific net length in m/ha |
| $A_{proj}$ | total project area in m² |

**Uses results of:** [EQ-3.4a](eq-03-04a_specific-net-length.md)

**User inputs:** [in-02](../01_input-project-data/in-02_total-project-area.md), [in-08](../01_input-project-data/in-08_network-length.md)
