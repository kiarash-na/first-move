# EQ-3.2d · Total Annual Cooling Demand

$$
Q_{cool,total} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{p=1}^{m} Q_{cool,i,p}
$$

| Symbol | Definition (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| $Q_{cool,total}$ | total annual heating demand of all buildings in kWh/a |
| $n$ | number of building types |
| $m$ | number of construction periods |
