# EQ-3.6b · Cooling Supply Coverage

$$
C_{cool} = \frac{Q_{cool,supply}}{Q_{cool,total}}
$$

| Symbol | Definition (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| $C_{cool}$ | cooling demand coverage |
| $Q_{cool,supply}$ | annual cooling supply per source in kWh/a |
| $Q_{cool,total}$ | total annual cooling demand of all buildings in kWh/a |
