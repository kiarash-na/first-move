# EQ-3.10f · Annualized Costs

$$
C_{annual} = C_{overall} \cdot a
$$

| Symbol | Definition (verbatim) |
|---|---|
| $C_{annual}$ | annualized costs in €/a |
| $C_{overall}$ | overall investment costs of the system in € |
| $a$ | annuity factor |

**Uses results of:** [EQ-3.10d](eq-03-10d_overall-investment.md), [EQ-3.10e](eq-03-10e_annuity-factor.md)
