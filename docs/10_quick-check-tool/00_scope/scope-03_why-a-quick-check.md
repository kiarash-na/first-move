# Why 5GDHC – and Why a Quick Check?

- **Energy Efficiency**: 5GDHC enables bidirectional heat exchange between buildings and efficiently integrates renewable energy and waste heat.
- **Sustainability**: Reduces reliance on fossil fuels.
- **Energy Resilience**: Uses local energy sources and thermal storage to improve energy security and flexibility.

> **THE NEED:** Give municipalities, energy service providers and other project initiators a first assessment of the technical and economic potential of 5GDHC networks
