# Overall result levels

> The overall result shall be communicated through a simple overall assessment such as:

- Promising
- Further analysis required
- Not promising

> Since the tool provides only a pre-assessment, the indicator is intentionally broad to avoid false precision and to clearly communicate that the result is a general initial assessment, not a detailed feasibility study.

Slides (slide 4):

> A broad overall indicator avoids false precision while it helps users understand what matters

- **PROMISING**: Favorable conditions identified
- **FURTHER ANALYSIS REQUIRED**: Mixed conditions, uncertainties or missing information
- **NOT PROMISING**: Unfavorable conditions identified
