# Overall Assessment Rule

> The overall assessment combines technical supply coverage, spatial suitability and economic viability. The purpose is to provide a transparent initial screening result rather than a definitive feasibility decision. The tool shall provide one of three overall results.

Table 11: Overall Result Classification: [table-11_overall-result-classification.yaml](../_reference/table-11_overall-result-classification.yaml)

> The overall assessment is a preliminary screening result and does not replace a detailed technical or economic feasibility study.
