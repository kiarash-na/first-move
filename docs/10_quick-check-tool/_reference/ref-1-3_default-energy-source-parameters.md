# Default Energy Source Parameters

> The following tables provide predefined annual energy supply potentials for different energy sources at three levels: low, medium and high. They also specify the corresponding coefficients of performance (COP) where applicable. The available energy sources are described below. The capacities shown in the following tables are the energy extracted from the source and NOT the thermal energy delivered to the building. Therefore, the COP has to be considered, as shown in Section 3.
>
> A dash in the tables indicates that no default capacity is provided for that source and application.
