# OQ-20 · Range boundaries in the classification tables

**Observation:** Ranges such as "90-99.9%", "70-89.9%", "10-30", "0.5-1.5", "0.8-1.0" / "0.3-0.8" leave inclusivity open, leave gaps (e.g. 99.95 %) or share a boundary (0.8). Table 11 uses "at least 90%" and "between 70%-90%".

**Source quotes:**

- Table 7: "Above or equal 100% / 90-99.9% / 70-89.9% / Below 70%"
- Table 8: "Above 30 buildings/ha / 10-30 buildings/ha / Below 10 buildings/ha"
- Table 9: "Above 1.5 / 0.5-1.5 / Below 0.5"
- Table 10: "0.8-1.0 / 0.3-0.8 / Below 0.3"
- Table 11, PROMISING: "All relevant heating and cooling demands have at least 90% annual supply coverage, the economic assessment is favorable and building density and heat density are both not classified as low."
- Table 11, FURTHER ANALYSIS REQUIRED: "Essential information is missing or uncertain, coverage is between 70%-90%, heat and building density indicators are mixed or the technical and economic results do not support a clear conclusion. If the project area includes industry."
