# OQ-01 · Percent values used directly in equations

**Observation:** Shares, interest rate and PV reduction are given in %, while the equations use them as factors without a stated conversion. N_i,p is not stated to be an integer.

**Source quotes:**

- EQ-3.1 glossary: $s_{i,p}$ … Share of building type i and construction period p in %
- Table 6: `Interest rate | 3 | %`
- Table 6: `PV cost reduction | 30 | %`
- §1.3: "Photovoltaic (PV) systems can be considered as an additional feature. Its potential effect on electricity costs shall be incorporated with an applicable reduction factor of 30%."
