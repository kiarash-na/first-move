# OQ-06 · PV: input and application of the 30 % reduction

**Observation:** PV is not listed in §2 Required Inputs. The 30 % reduction is stated for "electricity costs" without a formula; §3.11 only says "Consider a cost reduction, when PV is available."

**Source quotes:**

- §1.3: "Photovoltaic (PV) systems can be considered as an additional feature. Its potential effect on electricity costs shall be incorporated with an applicable reduction factor of 30%."
- Table 6: `PV cost reduction | 30 | %`
- §3.11: "Consider a cost reduction, when PV is available."
