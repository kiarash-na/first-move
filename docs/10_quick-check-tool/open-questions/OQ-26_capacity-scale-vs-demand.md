# OQ-26 · Source capacities are small compared with default demand

**Observation:** The highest capacity per source is 150,000 kWh/a (TBL-4/5). With the defaults of TBL-1 × TBL-3, one residential building needs about 30,000-68,000 kWh/a of heat, so coverage drops below 70 % for most projects above a handful of buildings. Whether the capacities are absolute per project, per building or per area is not stated.
