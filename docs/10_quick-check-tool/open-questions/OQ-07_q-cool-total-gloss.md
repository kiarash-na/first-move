# OQ-07 · Q_cool,total glossed as heating demand in §3.2

**Observation:** The glossary under the Q_cool,total equation says "total annual heating demand"; §3.6 glosses the same symbol as "total annual cooling demand".

**Source quotes:**

- EQ-3.2d glossary (§3.2): $Q_{cool,total}$ … total annual heating demand of all buildings in kWh/a
- EQ-3.6b glossary (§3.6): $Q_{cool,total}$ … total annual cooling demand of all buildings in kWh/a
