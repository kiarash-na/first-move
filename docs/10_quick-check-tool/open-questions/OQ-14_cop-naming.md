# OQ-14 · COP definition and COP_H / COP_C

**Observation:** Glossaries say "Coefficient of power of heat pump per source"; §1.3 says "coefficients of performance (COP)". EQ-3.11 uses COP_H and COP_C, which have no own glossary entry.

**Source quotes:**

- EQ-3.8a glossary: $COP$ … Coefficient of power of heat pump per source
- §1.3: "They also specify the corresponding coefficients of performance (COP) where applicable."
- EQ-3.11: $C_{el} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{Q_{heat,supply}}{COP_{H}} \cdot c_{el} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{Q_{cool,supply}}{COP_{C}} \cdot c_{el}$
