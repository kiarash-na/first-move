# OQ-21 · "Economic assessment favorable / clearly unfavorable" is not defined

**Observation:** Table 11 relies on an economic assessment; no rule or threshold relates R_overall, C_annual, C_el and C_O&M to "favorable" or "clearly unfavorable".

**Source quotes:**

- Table 11, PROMISING: "All relevant heating and cooling demands have at least 90% annual supply coverage, the economic assessment is favorable and building density and heat density are both not classified as low."
- Table 11, NOT PROMISING: "The economic assessment is clearly unfavorable, or relevant supply coverage is below 70% and both building density and heat density are low."
- §4.5: "The overall assessment combines technical supply coverage, spatial suitability and economic viability."
