## Decisions and small deviations

- D-39 to D-46 are in [owner decisions](2026-10-24_owner-decisions.md).
- **Language-aware tokens.** `{{operator.address}}`, `{{operator.country}}`, `{{authority.address}}` and `{{mail.region}}` read "Deutschland" / "EU-Region" on German pages.
- **DE files carry `updated` and `version`**, equal to EN (a test enforces it), so the DE review list shows the legal areas as complete.
- **The e2e suites start with the notice dismissed** (`playwright.config.ts` `storageState`), so the fixed notice never covers a click; `e2e/legal.spec.ts` opts out and tests it. Manually created contexts (`browser.newContext`) do not inherit it.
- **The cookie notice is informational only.** First Move sets no optional cookie, so a reject button would be misleading. It stays hidden on the server and until an effect has read its flag (`first-move:notice-ack`), so no storage is read during render.
- **Dashboard:** the notice is offset past the 18 rem sidebar from 900 px. The dashboard has no legal links (not approved).
- **The Cookie Policy lists `first-move:probe`**, a test entry the storage layer writes and removes at once (found by the drafting pass; not in the original inventory).
- **Landing footer:** the existing product nav is hidden below 1100 px, so the legal links are a separate full-width row (`nav.footer-legal`) that stays visible.
- **The Quick Check results and the calculations are untouched.** Legal text says so where it matters.

## Facts checked on 2026-10-24 (web)

- A P.O. box does not satisfy the address duty in § 5 DDG (IHK and law-firm guidance), see the risks.
- AI Act Art. 50 transparency applies since 2 August 2026. The Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) moved Annex III high-risk to 2 December 2027 and softened Art. 4; sources disagree on its publication date, so the pages avoid the changed wording ("measures to promote AI literacy" holds under both). Art. 2(12) does not exempt Art. 50 systems.
- The EU ODR platform closed on 20 July 2025; the Imprint says so and links nothing.
- Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. **Not confirmed:** Resend's DPA, SCCs and EU-region claims (one secondary source lists Resend Inc. as a US subprocessor under SCCs).
- Hamburg's data protection authority: Ludwig-Erhard-Str. 22, 20459 Hamburg (several consistent sources, not the official page).

Sources: [AI Act Omnibus overview](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html), [Article 4 after the Omnibus](https://ki-campus.org/en/blog/eu-ai-act-ai-omnibus-recalibrates-article-4-ai-literacy-remains-mandatory), [Art. 2(12) and open source](https://linuxfoundation.eu/newsroom/ai-act-explainer), [IHK guidance on the Impressum](https://www.ihk.de/duesseldorf/recht-und-steuern/recht/internetrecht/impressum-5109750), [ODR platform closure](https://www.ecc.fi/en/current/press-releases/the-european-online-dispute-resolution-odr-platform-will-be-discontinued-as-of-20-july-2025/), [Vercel DPF](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-is-now-certified-under-the-eu-us-data-privacy-framework-dpf).

## Risks the owner accepted (D-45)

1. **P.O. box only.** § 5 DDG asks for a serviceable street address; a P.O. box is not enough. Exposure: warning letters from competitors and fines.
2. **Abbreviated surname.** § 5 DDG asks for first name and surname.
3. **No second contact channel** beyond two e-mail addresses. (EU case law accepts e-mail plus another quick means of contact; two mailboxes are one medium.)
4. **No written proof** in the repo that the rights holders of the AQC methodology allow publication under Apache-2.0. The pages state that permission exists, because the owner said so.
5. **`NEXT_LOCALE` is set on the first language redirect**, before an explicit choice. The Cookie Policy says so plainly. Setting it only after a choice would need a change to `proxy.ts`, which was out of scope.
6. **No legal review** of any of the texts, and the German texts are marked as drafts.

## Points for a lawyer (raised while drafting)

- **Art. 50(1) for agent@.** The pages disclose that agent@ is answered by an AI system. Whether the operator counts as *provider* (a duty of Art. 50(1)) or *deployer* for that mailbox is not stated on any page.
- **English version prevails** (Terms, `severability`). For German consumers an unclear or one-sided language clause can be read against the drafter (§ 305c(2) BGB).
- **Transfer to a successor** (Terms, `successors`). A change of contract party normally needs the other side's consent; the clause gives 30 days' notice and a right to stop using the service. Check it against § 309 no. 10 BGB for consumers.
- **Consequential loss on the user** (Terms, `liability`). Check against § 307 BGB and the cardinal-duty rule.
- **"If we learn ... we will remove the link"** (Terms and Imprint) is a commitment. Confirm you want it.
- **Annex III point 2 reasoning** (AI page): First Move is not a safety component of heating infrastructure. Have it confirmed when the AI features are built.
- **Rights requests and statutory retention** rest on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR, which was not on the citable list; the Privacy Policy cites only (b) and (f).
- **German "Betreiber"** means the *deployer* in the AI Act. The German texts say "wir" for the operator and use "Betreiber" only for deployers.
- **DPAs.** The Privacy Policy calls Vercel and Resend processors under Art. 28. Vercel's DPA applies by plan; confirm one is in place for both.
- **E-mail tracking (D-63).** Resend adds open and click tracking to every e-mail sent from `mail.firstmove.energy`. German authorities generally treat tracking pixels and tracked links in e-mails as needing consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG). The Privacy Policy (1.1) discloses the tracking and relies on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Check this, or switch tracking off for both inboxes.
