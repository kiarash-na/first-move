# v0.2.0: legal pages, licence and cookie notice

Phase 12 on top of [v0.1](2026-10-24_v0.1.md). `package.json` is at 0.2.0. **No change to app behaviour** (D-39); D-38 (no further building without the owner) is lifted for this work only.

**New:** Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Imprint, AI transparency and Licence pages in EN and DE (`/en/terms` and so on), a legal footer on the landing and docs pages, a bottom-left cookie notice on all three layouts, `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0) and `NOTICE`.

**Run locally:** as for v0.1, then open `/en/terms`, `/en/privacy`, `/en/cookies`, `/en/imprint`, `/en/ai`, `/en/license` (and `/de/...`). The first visit to any page shows the cookie notice.

**Before launch:** the [pre-launch checklist](2026-10-24_phase-12_legal.md#pre-launch-checklist). The texts are not legally reviewed, the Imprint has a P.O. box and an abbreviated surname (D-45), and the German texts are drafts.

**Not in v0.2:** legal links inside the dashboard, the AI features (D-05), the PDF report (D-09), public docs content (D-22), a tag.
