## Notes on the choices

- **Logo.** No logo or brand skill is in the owner's skill catalog, so the mark follows the usual rules of a good mark: a simple geometry on a grid, legible at 16 px (the favicon drops the board points), works in one colour, only the two brand signals (cyan and orange, DESIGN-TOKENS §1), sharp corners. The animation is decorative and short: every animation ends (a never-ending glow also kept an e2e wait from finishing, and WCAG 2.2.2 asks for moving content to stop), and nothing moves with reduced motion. Each mark gets its own SVG filter id (`useId`).
- **Partner strip.** Moving content needs a way to stop it (WCAG 2.2.2), so besides pausing on hover and focus there is a Pause button. The sliding copies are hidden from assistive technology and out of the tab order; keyboard focus sends the row back to its start, so the focused card is always visible (WCAG 2.4.11). With reduced motion it is a static row. The contact page is linked over https (the http address the owner gave redirects there).
- **German default.** "German first, always": the browser language no longer decides; a saved English choice still wins. This means fewer request headers are read, and the Privacy and Cookie Policies were updated to say so (version 1.2).
- **Translation.** Done by subagents in two lanes (D-62: at most two at a time), each batch in its own worktree, each reviewed by a second agent against the English, then one consistency pass across all batches. The German is a draft for the owner's review; the legal texts stay under D-44 ("draft, not legally reviewed"). The email agent still writes in English (`lib/mail-agent` loads the English copy; the docs say so).

## German terms for the owner to confirm

The translators and reviewers settled every term but these, which are the owner's call:

- **Heat density** (MWh per metre of network) is „Wärmedichte“; German heat planning also says „Wärmeliniendichte“. The landing's illustrative area input uses „Wärmebedarfsdichte“, and the Business Plan prompts „Wärmebelegungsdichte“ (heat line density).
- **Energy price** (TBL-6) is the tariff term „Arbeitspreis“, next to „Grundpreis“; the electricity price parameter (€/kWh) is „Strompreis“, the electricity costs (€/a) „Stromkosten“.
- **Capacity per source** (TBL-4/5, kWh/a) is „Heizkapazität“ / „Kühlkapazität“, and delivered supply „Angebot“; industry may prefer „Wärmepotenzial“ or „Wärmeertrag“.
- **Vienna heating plan** categories in the Business Plan prompts: the official German names could not be checked (`prompts.heatingPlan`).
- **Email agent:** its notices are translated, but `lib/mail-agent` still writes in English, as the docs say; whether German answers by email work depends on A07.
