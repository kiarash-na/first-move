# 5GDHC Quick Check Tool: Kickoff Slides

## Slide 1

**5GDHC Quick Check Tool**

Turn an existing assessment method into a practical digital tool for evaluating 5GDHC potentials

## Slide 2: Why 5GDHC – and Why a Quick Check?

- **Energy Efficiency**: 5GDHC enables bidirectional heat exchange between buildings and efficiently integrates renewable energy and waste heat.
- **Sustainability**: Reduces reliance on fossil fuels.
- **Energy Resilience**: Uses local energy sources and thermal storage to improve energy security and flexibility.

**THE NEED:** Give municipalities, energy service providers and other project initiators a first assessment of the technical and economic potential of 5GDHC networks

## Slide 3: Build a Useful First Assessment Tool

Translate the provided concept into a functional digital prototype for a web application

1. **Enter Project Data**: Area, buildings, demand, sources and other inputs
2. **Run Quick Check**: Apply the provided methodology and assumptions
3. **Review Assessment**: Overall indicator, key drivers and potential hurdles
4. **Export Results**: Create a report for discussion and next steps

**DESIGN PRINCIPLE:** Communicate an initial indication, not a definitive feasibility decision

## Slide 4: Make the Result Simple and Transparent

A broad overall indicator avoids false precision while it helps users understand what matters

- **PROMISING**: Favorable conditions identified
- **FURTHER ANALYSIS REQUIRED**: Mixed conditions, uncertainties or missing information
- **NOT PROMISING**: Unfavorable conditions identified

**OVERALL ASSESSMENT:** Show the main drivers: e.g., heat-demand density, local heat sources and investment costs

## Slide 5: Deliver a Clear And Useful Prototype

Focus on a working and understandable implementation of the existing concept

**In Scope**

- Functional prototype
- User-friendly interface
- Clear results and visualizations
- PDF or similar output

**Out of Scope**

- Creating an assessment methodology
- Adapting the calculation concept
- Detailed engineering

**DEMO IT LIVE:** Enter project data → Run the Quick Check → Explain the result → Show the report
