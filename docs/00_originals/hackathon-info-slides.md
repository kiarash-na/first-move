## Slide 2: Business Models for 5GDHC

From technical potential to viable and scalable business models.

During this hackathon, interdisciplinary teams will develop, test and refine innovative business models for concrete 5GDHC use cases.

## Slide 3: The Problem

5GDHC networks offer great potential for sustainable heating and cooling, but their implementation faces economic and organizational challenges.

Key questions could be:

- Who benefits, and who pays?
- Who owns and operates the network?
- How can customers be motivated to connect?
- How are investments, revenues and risks shared?
- How can successful models be scaled and replicated?

## Slide 4: The Challenge

**Turn Ideas into Viable Business Models**
Develop, test and pitch a realistic and scalable business model for a 5GDHC network.

- Select a provided scenario or develop your own use case.
- Explore different approaches using the Business Model Canvas
- Identify Customers, partners, financing and ownership structures
- Test economic feasibility and practical implementation
- Evaluate opportunities for replication and scalability

## Slide 5: Goals

**Turn Ideas into Viable Business Models**
Develop, test and pitch a realistic and scalable business model for a 5GDHC network.

- Develop innovative Business Model Canvases tailored to 5GDHC.
- Assess economic feasibility and practical applicability.
- Improve concepts through expert feedback and mentoring.
- Identify realistic pathways towards implementation.
- Explore opportunities for replication and scalability.

## Slide 6: Working Method & final Pitch

From Concept to Implementation.

1. **Define the Opportunity**: Identify your use case, customers, energy needs and key challenges.
2. **Build the Business Model**: Develop and compare different Business Model Canvases.
3. **Reality Check**: Test economic assumptions, identify risks and assess feasibility.
4. **Improve & Pitch**: Refine your model, define next steps and present your solution.

Suggested approach – feel free to develop your own way of working!
