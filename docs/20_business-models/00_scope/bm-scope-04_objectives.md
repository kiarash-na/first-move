# Challenge Objectives

> Challenge Objectives:

- Develop and critically assess business models tailored to 5GDHC networks.
- Evaluate their economic feasibility, practical applicability and implementation potential.
- Refine business models through expert input, mentoring and iterative feedback.
- Identify key opportunities, barriers and critical success factors.
- Explore the scalability and replicability of promising business models across different geographical and organizational contexts
