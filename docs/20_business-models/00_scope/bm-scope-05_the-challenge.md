# The Challenge

> Teams are challenged to develop and present a convincing, evidence-based business model for a 5GDHC network based on a specific use case.
>
> The focus is on creating a realistic value proposition, identifying relevant stakeholders, developing suitable ownership and financing structures, and critically assessing economic feasibility and implementation potential.
>
> Teams may develop a new business model, refine an existing approach, or compare different models to identify the most promising solution.
>
> Particular attention should be given to scalability, replicability and the conditions required for successful implementation.
>
> Teams can choose from predefined scenarios or propose their own use case aligned with the challenge objectives.

Only in V01:

> Solutions may include innovative partnerships, tariff structures, ownership models or approaches to demand aggregation.
