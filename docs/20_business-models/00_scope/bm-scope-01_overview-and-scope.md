# Overview and Scope

> How can 5th Generation District Heating and Cooling (5GDHC) networks evolve from technically promising concepts into economically viable, scalable and sustainable business models?
>
> During this hackathon, interdisciplinary teams will develop, test and refine business models for 5GDHC networks. The focus is on customer needs, value creation, ownership structures, financing and practical implementation rather than detailed engineering design.
>
> Particular attention will be given to different geographical and settlement conditions, including existing neighbourhoods, rural areas and locations.
>
> A key objective is to explore the scalability and replicability of different business models.

> Building on the findings and insights gained from the research project "Anergie Leicht Gemacht", this hackathon aims to explore and develop viable, scalable and replicable business models for the practical implementation of 5GDHC networks.
