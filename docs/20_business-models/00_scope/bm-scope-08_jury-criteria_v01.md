# Proposed jury evaluation criteria (V01)

In V01 these items follow the bullet "Proposed jury evaluation criteria" inside the "Final pitch" list.

- Problem–solution fit and clarity of the use case.
- Business-model coherence and stakeholder incentives.
- Economic plausibility and realistic implementation pathway.
- Quality of risk analysis, assumptions and validation approach.
- Innovation, scalability and clarity of the final pitch.
