# Key questions

> Key questions include:

1. Who benefits and who pays?
2. Who owns and operates the network?
3. What motivates customers to connect?
4. How are investments, revenues and risks distributed?
5. How do energy prices, funding, regulation and long-term commitments affect feasibility?
6. How can 5GDHC business models contribute to resilient local energy infrastructure and energy security?
7. What risks, dependencies and operational responsibilities need to be considered to ensure a reliable and secure energy supply?
