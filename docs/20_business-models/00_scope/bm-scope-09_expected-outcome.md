# Expected Outcome

> The goal is to develop the most convincing and feasible business model, considering not only its economic viability but also its scalability, replicability and real-world implementation potential.
>
> Equally important is the critical assessment of opportunities, challenges and limitations. Teams should clearly identify what works, what doesn't, and why, supporting their conclusions with well-founded arguments.
>
> A successful outcome is not necessarily a perfect business model, but a convincing, realistic and critically evaluated approach that demonstrates a clear understanding of its implementation potential and limitations.

V01 "Expected Outcomes" list:

- A concise definition of the use case: target audience, problem statement, needs, and local context.
- Work with the Business Model Canvas—analysis based on the Business Model Canvas
- What works? What doesn’t work? Can these models be scaled? If so, where? If not, why not?
- A simplified assessment of feasibility and implementability: costs/revenue, operating and ownership models, financing, and incentives for stakeholders.
- Final presentation
