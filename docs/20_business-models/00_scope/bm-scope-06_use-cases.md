# Possible use cases

> Possible use cases: Here, you can develop various Business Model Canvases, compare them, refine them, or find new solutions. What works, what doesn’t, and where are the opportunities for scalability? Predefined scenarios are available, and you can also develop your own.
>
> The following points may be taken into consideration:

- Existing neighbourhood or district refurbishment: connecting multiple buildings with differing heating and cooling needs.
- Waste-heat integration: using industrial, commercial or other local low-temperature heat sources.
- Applications in rural areas or on the outskirts of cities: Economic and organizational feasibility of low-density networks.
- Teams may select a provided scenario or propose a comparable use case, subject to challenge-track alignment.
