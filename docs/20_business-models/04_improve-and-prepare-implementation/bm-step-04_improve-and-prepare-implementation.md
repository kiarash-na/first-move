# 4 | Improve & prepare implementation

1. Refine your business model based on the Reality Check, expert feedback and identified challenges.
2. Assess its potential for scalability and replication under different geographical, economic and organisational conditions.
3. Outline the next practical steps, including data requirements, stakeholder engagement, potential partnerships, pilot opportunities and key decisions needed to move towards implementation.

V01 wording:

- Incorporate mentor feedback into the models – work on different Canvas, develop them.
- Set out the next practical steps: data to collect, stakeholders to engage, partnerships to secure, pilot opportunities and a realistic decision gate for progressing the concept.
